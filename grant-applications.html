Funding & Grant Applications

Particular Expertise in Pobal Funding Applications, Backed by 20 Years of Experience Within Pobal

GET IN TOUCH VIEW PRICING

Structured Training for Funding Success

The Grant Application & Business Plan Masterclass Programme is designed to build the capacity of community groups, social enterprises, SICAP target groups, LEADER project promoters, and voluntary boards seeking funding.

Nurture Impact has particular expertise in Pobal funding applications, drawing on 20 years of experience working within Pobal and a detailed understanding of the funding environment and application process. Each session includes branded materials, participant handouts, practical templates aligned with Pobal and funder expectations, real-world examples, and follow-up resources.

Funding & Grant Applications is one part of Nurture Impact's wider consultancy offering. See all services →

Who Is This For?

Designed for organisations at every stage of their funding journey

Community Groups

LDC-supported groups, voluntary boards, and committees preparing funding applications.

Social Enterprises

Trading organisations seeking LEADER, Rethink Ireland, or other social enterprise funding.

SICAP & LEADER

Target groups and project promoters working with Local Development Companies.

Programme Modules

Learning Outcome

Understand how funders and appraisers assess applications

Participants will gain insight into the criteria, scoring systems, and decision-making processes that determine which projects receive funding.

Core Content

  • How funders prioritise and score applications
  • Common appraisal criteria and scoring rubrics
  • Red flags that trigger concerns for reviewers
  • The decision-making process from submission to approval

Learning Outcome

Build strong, evidence-based needs analyses

Participants will learn how to source and present credible data so that their applications clearly demonstrate the local need and justify project investment.

Core Content

  • Sources of credible local and national data
  • How to conduct meaningful consultations
  • Aligning needs with funder priorities
  • Presenting evidence clearly and persuasively

Learning Outcome

Design realistic, fundable activities

Participants will practice translating identified needs into clear, structured actions that align with funder priorities and demonstrate feasibility.

Core Content

  • Linking activities directly to identified needs
  • Making activities specific, measurable, and achievable
  • Realistic timelines and resource allocation
  • Demonstrating alignment with programme objectives

Learning Outcome

Prepare transparent, defensible budgets

Participants will understand how to build budgets that show value for money, connect directly to planned activities, and withstand appraisal scrutiny.

Core Content

  • Understanding eligible and ineligible costs
  • Demonstrating value for money
  • Linking budget lines to specific activities
  • Preparing for budget queries during appraisal

Learning Outcome

Write measurable outcomes and impact statements

Participants will be equipped to define outputs, outcomes, and indicators that show meaningful change and support strong monitoring and evaluation.

Core Content

  • Distinguishing outputs, outcomes, and impact
  • Setting SMART indicators
  • Aligning with programme-level indicators
  • Building in monitoring and evaluation from the start

Learning Outcome

Demonstrate governance, capacity, and risk management

Participants will learn how to present organisational structures, policies, and risk controls that strengthen funder confidence in delivery ability.

Core Content

  • Presenting governance structures clearly
  • Demonstrating organisational capacity
  • Identifying and mitigating project risks
  • Building funder confidence in delivery

Learning Outcome

Produce high-quality business plans for social enterprises

Participants will understand how to structure a business plan that integrates financial projections, market need, and social impact in a clear, funder-aligned way.

Core Content

  • Business plan structure for social enterprises
  • Market analysis and competitive positioning
  • Financial projections and sustainability
  • Integrating social impact throughout

Learning Outcome

Improve funding readiness and organisational sustainability

Participants will gain skills and tools that strengthen long-term planning, reduce reliance on ad-hoc funding, and improve organisational resilience.

Core Content

  • How appraisers read and score applications
  • Assessing clarity, consistency, and coherence
  • Evaluating feasibility and evidence
  • Common weaknesses and how to address them

Core Content

  • Writing for busy readers
  • Structuring responses for clarity
  • Avoiding jargon and vague language
  • Using headings, bullets, and formatting effectively

Tools & Takeaways

  • Writing style guide
  • Before/after application examples
  • Self-editing checklist

Core Content

  • Pre-submission quality assurance steps
  • Checking for consistency and completeness
  • Common last-minute errors to avoid
  • Document preparation and submission tips

Tools & Takeaways

  • Comprehensive submission checklist
  • Quality assurance template
  • Final review workflow
